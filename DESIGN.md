# Refund Court visual direction

The user delegated decisions and limited work to desktop. Operate mode: reviewers inspect a promise, run a paid request, then inspect exact evidence and settlement.

Grounded candidates considered: financial dispute ledger, developer network inspector, consumer refund desk, infrastructure status console, laboratory notebook, settlement statement, agent execution trace. The assigned fourth direction is the infrastructure status console: calm, high-contrast navigation; a wide evidence workbench; meaningful state color; persistent route, amount and chain labels. It wins on product clarity and familiarity for agent builders. The generated art and ephemera challengers lose both axes; borrow their rigor of information density, never their decorative shapes. Wayfinding's persistent orientation raises the clarity of chain and transaction state.

Use a warm off-white workspace, deep pine navigation and ink, restrained rust for breach, green for paid/refunded. Space Grotesk headings, DM Sans prose, system monospace only for hashes and payloads. Wide split view with a concise left case list and a substantial right evidence reader. Hairline rules, generous whitespace between tasks, no decorative stats. A precise square court mark is geometric identity, not a legal illustration. Desktop minimum width 1100px; layouts reviewed at 1280 and 1440 only.

Recorded proof is a distinct view with a capture timestamp and receipts. Live chain reads have a separate refresh timestamp and explicit failures. Empty data remains empty. The signature interaction selects a criterion and exposes its exact quote in the signed response alongside the payment and native payout receipts.
